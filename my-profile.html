Kuwait Express Deliveries

We provide localized white-glove courier dispatch across all 6 Kuwait Governorates: Al Ahmadi, Capital (Al Asimah), Hawally, Farwaniya, Mubarak Al-Kabeer, and Al Jahra.

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of atelier confirmation. Delivery privileges are free on orders exceeding KD 20.000.

Self-Service Order Cancellation

Orders in the initial 🟡 Pending Dispatch stage can be cancelled self-service with zero fees. Once an order enters 🟠 Atelier Tailoring or 🔵 Out for Delivery, our concierges process it directly to avoid garment damage.

Returns & Exchanges Guarantee

Pieces in pristine, unworn condition with atelier tags attached may be exchanged or credited within 14 days of delivery. Custom darra alterations are final sale.
