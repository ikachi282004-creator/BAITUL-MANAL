Exchange & Refund Policy

In accordance with Kuwait Consumer Protection Law (Law No. 39/2014)

1. Standard Exchange Window

Under Kuwait Consumer Protection Law, customers may request an exchange or return within 14 calendar days from the delivery timestamp, provided the item meets our condition criteria.

2. Item Condition Requirements

  • The garment must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and completely free of perfume, makeup, or scent.
  • All original Baitul Manal atelier tags, ribbons, and internal labels must remain fully intact.
  • The product must be enclosed in its original clear boutique garment cover.

3. Hygiene & Non-Returnable Items

Under Ministry of Health & Consumer Protection guidelines, Baby Innerwear and items marked as Final Sale Clearance cannot be returned once delivered.

4. Return Channels & Refunds

  • Atelier Drop-off: Return items free of logistics fees at Shop 133 & 134, Fahaheel Bazar, Kuwait.
  • Home Courier Collection: A flat courier retrieval fee of KD 1.500 to KD 2.000 applies based on your governorate.
  • Settlement: K-Net and Card transactions will be reversed to the issuing bank within 3–7 business days. COD purchases are reimbursed via store credit voucher or direct bank transfer.

5. Initiating an Exchange

Contact our concierge team via WhatsApp at +965 6045 4629 with your receipt reference ID and photos of the item.
