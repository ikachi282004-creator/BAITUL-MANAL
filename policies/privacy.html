Privacy & Data Protection

Kuwait Electronic Transactions & Consumer Privacy Guidelines • 2026

1. Information Collected

We collect essential order fulfillment coordinates including recipient name, Kuwait mobile telephone number (+965), designated delivery governorate, district area, block, street, and residential building number[cite: 5]. Email addresses are optional and utilized solely for PDF receipt transmission[cite: 5].

2. Payment Security

Baitul Manal does not store or process debit card PINs or CVV numbers on internal servers. All K-Net debit transactions and card clearances are executed via 256-bit encrypted bank gateways adhering to Central Bank of Kuwait electronic security standards[cite: 5].

3. WhatsApp Concierge Communication

When you choose to dispatch orders or submit inquiries through WhatsApp, your phone number and order summary are shared with our Fahaheel atelier team (+965 6045 4629) exclusively for delivery coordination and fitting advice.

4. Client Data Control

Shopping preferences and active bag records are stored locally on your device via browser storage (`localStorage`). You can clear your cart and wishlist history anytime directly through your device settings or on-site buttons.
